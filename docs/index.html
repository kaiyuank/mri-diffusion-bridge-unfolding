Diffusion-Bridge Unfolding for MRI Reconstruction

Recovering MRI images from undersampled k-space using diffusion-bridge priors and model-based consistency updates.

Sam Kang

Advised by Professor Ulugbek Kamilov

Computational Imaging Group · Washington University in St. Louis

01 · Project overview

Abstract

This project combines an image-to-image diffusion bridge with deep unfolding for MRI reconstruction. Starting from an image formed from undersampled k-space, the bridge provides a learned prior for recovering the clean image. A time-dependent operator derived from the bridge mean provides a consistency update in image space. Experiments compared this approach with unfolding using the MRI forward operator and with a standalone diffusion bridge.

02 · Acquisition model

Background

An undersampled MRI acquisition can be modeled as

y=Ax0+e,A=PFS,x1=A†y.y = A x_0 + e, \qquad A = PFS, \qquad x_1 = A^\dagger y.

Here, x0x_0 is the reference image, yy is the acquired k-space, and ee is measurement noise. The operator SS applies coil sensitivities, FF is the Fourier transform, and PP selects acquired samples. The fixed linear map A†A^\dagger forms the degraded image initializer x1x_1. The notation AHA^{\mathrm H} denotes the Hermitian adjoint.

Deep unfolding alternates a consistency step from the acquisition model with a learned image update. An image-to-image diffusion bridge learns a restoration process between the degraded and clean endpoints. This project uses the bridge as a learned prior and adds an explicit consistency step to guide the reconstruction. The bridge formulation follows I²SB by Liu et al. (ICML 2023).

03 · Reconstruction method

Derivation

Unfolding with the MRI forward operator

The conventional data fidelity term measures disagreement with acquired k-space. Initialize x^0=x1\hat{x}_0=x_1 and define

LA(x)=12∥Ax−y∥22.L_A(x)=\tfrac12\lVert Ax-y\rVert_2^2.

At unfolding step kk, a gradient step is followed by a learned image update.

zk=x^k−γkAH(Ax^k−y),x^k+1=λkDθ(zk,t)+(1−λk)zk.\begin{aligned} z_k &= \hat{x}_k-\gamma_k A^{\mathrm H}(A\hat{x}_k-y),\\ \hat{x}_{k+1} &= \lambda_k D_\theta(z_k,t)+(1-\lambda_k)z_k. \end{aligned}

Here, γk\gamma_k is the gradient step size, λk\lambda_k controls the blend, and Dθ(⋅,t)D_\theta(\cdot,t) is the learned image map conditioned on diffusion time tt.

Unfolding with the bridge operator

The bridge mean at time tt is a weighted average of the clean and degraded endpoints.

st=αtx0+(1−αt)x1,αt∈[0,1].s_t = \alpha_t x_0+(1-\alpha_t)x_1, \qquad \alpha_t\in[0,1].

Substituting x1=A†(Ax0+e)x_1=A^\dagger(Ax_0+e) gives an effective forward model for the bridge mean.

st=αtx0+(1−αt)A†(Ax0+e)=[αtI+(1−αt)A†A]x0+(1−αt)A†e=Htx0+wt,\begin{aligned} s_t &= \alpha_t x_0+(1-\alpha_t)A^\dagger(Ax_0+e)\\ &= \big[\alpha_t I+(1-\alpha_t)A^\dagger A\big]x_0 +(1-\alpha_t)A^\dagger e\\ &= H_t x_0+w_t, \end{aligned}

where Ht=αtI+(1−αt)A†AH_t=\alpha_t I+(1-\alpha_t)A^\dagger A and wt=(1−αt)A†ew_t=(1-\alpha_t)A^\dagger e. The term wtw_t comes from the original measurement noise. The derivation describes the bridge mean, while a sampled bridge state also contains diffusion noise.

At fixed tt, let s^t\hat{s}_t be the estimate of the bridge mean used in the unfolding stage. The bridge consistency term and updates are

LH,t(x)=12∥Htx−s^t∥22,L_{H,t}(x)=\tfrac12\lVert H_t x-\hat{s}_t\rVert_2^2,
zk=x^k−γkHtH(Htx^k−s^t),x^k+1=λkDθ(zk,t)+(1−λk)zk.\begin{aligned} z_k &= \hat{x}_k-\gamma_k H_t^{\mathrm H}(H_t\hat{x}_k-\hat{s}_t),\\ \hat{x}_{k+1} &= \lambda_k D_\theta(z_k,t)+(1-\lambda_k)z_k. \end{aligned}

The AA-based update matches acquired k-space. The HtH_t-based update matches an estimated bridge mean in image space.

04 · Evaluation

Experiments

Experiments varied the number of unfolding steps and compared three approaches.

  1. Standalone diffusion-bridge reconstruction.
  2. Deep unfolding with the MRI forward operator AA.
  3. Deep unfolding with the bridge operator HtH_t.
