01 · Project overview

Abstract

This project combines an image-to-image diffusion bridge with deep unfolding for MRI reconstruction. Starting from an image formed from undersampled k-space, the bridge provides a learned prior for recovering the clean image. A time-dependent operator derived from the bridge mean provides a consistency update in image space. Experiments compared this approach with unfolding using the MRI forward operator and with a standalone diffusion bridge. One recorded reconstruction achieved 33.04 dB PSNR, 0.9365 SSIM, and 0.0111 LPIPS.

02 · Acquisition model

Background

An undersampled MRI acquisition can be modeled as

y=Ax0+e,A=PFS,x1=A†y.y = A x_0 + e, \qquad A = PFS, \qquad x_1 = A^\dagger y.y=Ax0​+e,A=PFS,x1​=A†y.

Here, x0x_0x0​ is the reference image, yyy is the acquired k-space, and eee is measurement noise. The operator SSS applies coil sensitivities, FFF is the Fourier transform, and PPP selects acquired samples. The fixed linear map A†A^\daggerA† forms the degraded image initializer x1x_1x1​. The notation AHA^{\mathrm H}AH denotes the Hermitian adjoint.

Deep unfolding alternates a consistency step from the acquisition model with a learned image update. An image-to-image diffusion bridge learns a restoration process between the degraded and clean endpoints. This project uses the bridge as a learned prior and adds an explicit consistency step to guide the reconstruction. The bridge formulation follows I²SB by Liu et al. (ICML 2023).

03 · Reconstruction method

Derivation

Unfolding with the MRI forward operator

The conventional data fidelity term measures disagreement with acquired k-space. Initialize x^0=x1\hat{x}_0=x_1x^0​=x1​ and define

LA(x)=12∥Ax−y∥22.L_A(x)=\tfrac12\lVert Ax-y\rVert_2^2.LA​(x)=21​∥Ax−y∥22​.

At unfolding step kkk, a gradient step is followed by a learned image update.

zk=x^k−γkAH(Ax^k−y),x^k+1=λkDθ(zk,t)+(1−λk)zk.\begin{aligned} z_k &= \hat{x}_k-\gamma_k A^{\mathrm H}(A\hat{x}_k-y),\\ \hat{x}_{k+1} &= \lambda_k D_\theta(z_k,t)+(1-\lambda_k)z_k. \end{aligned}zk​x^k+1​​=x^k​−γk​AH(Ax^k​−y),=λk​Dθ​(zk​,t)+(1−λk​)zk​.​

Here, γk\gamma_kγk​ is the gradient step size, λk\lambda_kλk​ controls the blend, and Dθ(⋅,t)D_\theta(\cdot,t)Dθ​(⋅,t) is the learned image map conditioned on diffusion time ttt.

Unfolding with the bridge operator

The bridge mean at time ttt is a weighted average of the clean and degraded endpoints.

st=αtx0+(1−αt)x1,αt∈[0,1].s_t = \alpha_t x_0+(1-\alpha_t)x_1, \qquad \alpha_t\in[0,1].st​=αt​x0​+(1−αt​)x1​,αt​∈[0,1].

Substituting x1=A†(Ax0+e)x_1=A^\dagger(Ax_0+e)x1​=A†(Ax0​+e) gives an effective forward model for the bridge mean.

st=αtx0+(1−αt)A†(Ax0+e)=[αtI+(1−αt)A†A]x0+(1−αt)A†e=Htx0+wt,\begin{aligned} s_t &= \alpha_t x_0+(1-\alpha_t)A^\dagger(Ax_0+e)\\ &= \big[\alpha_t I+(1-\alpha_t)A^\dagger A\big]x_0 +(1-\alpha_t)A^\dagger e\\ &= H_t x_0+w_t, \end{aligned}st​​=αt​x0​+(1−αt​)A†(Ax0​+e)=[αt​I+(1−αt​)A†A]x0​+(1−αt​)A†e=Ht​x0​+wt​,​

where Ht=αtI+(1−αt)A†AH_t=\alpha_t I+(1-\alpha_t)A^\dagger AHt​=αt​I+(1−αt​)A†A and wt=(1−αt)A†ew_t=(1-\alpha_t)A^\dagger ewt​=(1−αt​)A†e. The term wtw_twt​ comes from the original measurement noise. The derivation describes the bridge mean, while a sampled bridge state also contains diffusion noise.

At fixed ttt, let s^t\hat{s}_ts^t​ be the estimate of the bridge mean used in the unfolding stage. The bridge consistency term and updates are

LH,t(x)=12∥Htx−s^t∥22,L_{H,t}(x)=\tfrac12\lVert H_t x-\hat{s}_t\rVert_2^2,LH,t​(x)=21​∥Ht​x−s^t​∥22​,
zk=x^k−γkHtH(Htx^k−s^t),x^k+1=λkDθ(zk,t)+(1−λk)zk.\begin{aligned} z_k &= \hat{x}_k-\gamma_k H_t^{\mathrm H}(H_t\hat{x}_k-\hat{s}_t),\\ \hat{x}_{k+1} &= \lambda_k D_\theta(z_k,t)+(1-\lambda_k)z_k. \end{aligned}zk​x^k+1​​=x^k​−γk​HtH​(Ht​x^k​−s^t​),=λk​Dθ​(zk​,t)+(1−λk​)zk​.​

The AAA-based update matches acquired k-space. The HtH_tHt​-based update matches an estimated bridge mean in image space.
